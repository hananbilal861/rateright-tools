How the tax set-aside is calculated

  1. Profit = yearly revenue − yearly expenses
  2. Tax estimate = profit × tax rate
  3. Set-aside % = tax estimate ÷ revenue

Because expenses reduce your taxable profit, the set-aside percentage of revenue is lower than your tax rate. Move that percentage into a separate savings account every time a client pays you.

Frequently asked questions

How much should a freelancer set aside for taxes?

Many freelancers save 20 to 30 percent of profit, but it depends on your country, income and deductions. Ask a local accountant for your exact rate.

Do I pay tax on revenue or on profit?

In most countries, on profit: revenue minus allowable business expenses. Rules differ, so check your local tax authority.

Should I keep tax money in a separate account?

Yes. Moving the set-aside percentage into savings whenever you get paid makes tax time much less stressful.
